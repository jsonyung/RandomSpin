📖 How It Works

Everything you need to know about assigning ads and leads fairly.

Task field

Enter Task / Reason to Call

Before you spin, type a short note about this lead — e.g. “Facebook ad lead” or “Missed call callback”. When the wheel stops, the result popup says “[Name] has been chosen for [your task]!” so the whole team sees who got it and why. Leave it blank and it will say “this lead” instead.

Recommended

Fair Bag — equal share, surprise order

Think of four cards in a bag. The app shuffles them, then draws one name per spin without putting it back.

  • After one full round, each person gets exactly 1 lead.
  • Then it shuffles a new bag — order is different every round.
  • Not a fixed loop — nobody can predict the whole list from spin 1.
  • Best for: equal ads while looking like the wheel decided.
Luck mode

Pure Random — fresh coin flip every spin

Each click is independent. Everyone has the same % chance every time, even if they just won.

  • Same person can win twice in a row.
  • Over a short stretch, counts may look uneven.
  • Best for: maximum “it was luck” with no rules.
Soft balance

Weighted — catch-up for whoever is behind

Still random, but the app remembers session win counts. People with fewer wins get a higher chance next spin; people ahead get lower chance but can still win.

  • Not a strict 1-each cycle — a soft balancer so nobody stays at zero.
  • Check the scoreboard under the wheel to see counts.
  • Best for: random feel without starving one agent.
Speed

Spin Speed

  • Quick (~1 sec) — busy moment, lead just came in.
  • Normal (~3.5 sec) — good show for the team.
  • Dramatic (~5.5 sec) — slow settle, extra fair look.

Speed only changes animation — it does not change who wins.

Scoreboard

Session tally

The numbers under the wheel show how many times each person has won this session (until you refresh or reset counts in Settings).
